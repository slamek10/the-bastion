# Models

All files here are made from four packs by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com), licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. They were slimmed with `scripts/build-models.mjs` (the people) and `scripts/build-kits.mjs` (the buildings and nature): roughness maps dropped, textures resized, unused pieces and clips removed.

The outfits (shirts, trousers, shoes in each agent's colours) are coloured in The Bastion's shader by skeleton region, and the accessories (tie, scarf, cap, apron, binoculars, badge) are built in code; neither comes from a pack.

Made in code, not from a pack: the ground, path and water textures, lamp posts, benches, barrels, the dock and the boat, the fountain, the meeting table and chairs, the forge's racks and antenna mast, the copper pipes, the clock face and every screen.
